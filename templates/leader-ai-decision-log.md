## Decisions to Log

- approving or blocking an AI tool
- moving a pilot into production
- approving a customer-facing AI workflow
- approving a high-risk use
- changing risk appetite
- enabling connected apps, agents, browser access, file access, voice, or screen access
- accepting a material risk
- retiring a tool
- changing training requirements

## Minimum Evidence

- outcome sought
- owner
- affected teams or people
- data classes involved
- tool/vendor reviewed
- controls in place
- human accountability
- review date
