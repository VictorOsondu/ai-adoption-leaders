# High-Risk AI Use Escalation

Last reviewed: 2026-07-24. See [sources and verification notes](../sources.md).

Use this when a proposed AI use could affect people, rights, money, safety, regulated activity, confidential data, or public trust.

## Trigger Questions

Escalate if AI may:

- affect hiring, performance, promotion, redundancy, discipline, pay, or access to work
- affect customers' eligibility, pricing, refunds, credit, insurance, services, or legal rights
- handle personal data at scale
- handle health, safeguarding, children's, financial, legal, biometric, or special-category data
- provide public advice or authoritative guidance
- make external commitments
- use autonomous agents or tools that act outside the organisation
- become part of a product or service sold to customers
- operate in a regulated sector

## Minimum Controls Before Approval

- named accountable owner
- data and privacy review
- legal or professional review where needed
- security review for connected systems or agents
- human oversight
- monitoring and incident route
- user training
- appeal or challenge route where people are affected
- review date and stop condition
