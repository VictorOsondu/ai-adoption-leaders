# Champion Cascade Plan

Last reviewed: 2026-07-24. See [sources and verification notes](../sources.md).

Use this to build adoption through trusted peers rather than mandates alone.

## Champion Role

A champion is not unpaid tech support. A champion helps the team learn safe, useful workflows and escalates risks or blockers.

## Selection Criteria

- trusted by peers
- one stage ahead, not necessarily an expert
- uses approved tools responsibly
- understands data and review rules
- willing to share examples
- has manager support and protected time

## Champion Responsibilities

- share one safe Improve workflow
- help peers apply ground rules
- collect questions and blockers
- report unsafe or unclear use
- feed useful examples into training
- avoid becoming the only person who can use AI

## Leadership Responsibilities

- give champions visible permission
- protect time
- define boundaries
- provide an escalation route
- recognise useful learning, not just output volume
- review what champions are seeing

## Review Questions

- Are champions active?
- Are questions increasing or decreasing for the right reasons?
- Are teams copying safe workflows?
- Are risks being surfaced early?
- Does any champion need support, replacement, or more time?
