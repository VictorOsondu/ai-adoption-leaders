# AI Risk Appetite Statement

Last reviewed: 2026-07-24. See [sources and verification notes](../sources.md).

Use this to define where the organisation will move quickly, where it will use controls, and where it will pause.

## Overall Position

Our AI risk appetite is:

- [ ] Conservative
- [ ] Balanced
- [ ] Ambitious

In one sentence, this means:

## Green: Move Quickly

Allowed with normal human review:

- non-sensitive internal drafting
- summarising public or approved internal material
- meeting-note drafting
- internal knowledge-base drafts
- workflow documentation
- first-draft communications that are reviewed before use

## Amber: Controlled Use

Allowed only with owner, controls, and review:

- client-facing or customer-facing output
- personal data in approved tools
- employee or HR-adjacent material
- financial, legal, compliance, or regulated claims
- tools connected to email, drives, CRM, finance, HR, browser, files, voice, screen, or agents
- workflow automation that changes records or triggers actions

## Red: Board or Executive Escalation

Requires explicit escalation before use:

- decisions affecting people, employment, access, eligibility, rights, money, health, safety, welfare, or services
- public advice or authoritative guidance
- regulated or high-risk AI systems
- AI making commitments, prices, refunds, discounts, policy exceptions, or legal/financial recommendations
- sensitive personal data, safeguarding, health, or protected-characteristic data
- autonomous agents with external actions
- material use in products or services sold to customers

## Prohibited Unless Lawfully and Separately Approved

- unlawful or deceptive uses
- biometric, surveillance, or profiling uses outside approved policy
- credential handling in AI tools
- unapproved processing of confidential, personal, or regulated data
- hidden AI decisions about people

## Review

Review this statement every six months and after any serious incident, major tool change, regulatory change, or new high-risk use case.
