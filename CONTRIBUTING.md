# Contributing

This playbook gets better when leaders who've actually run an adoption programme share what worked and what didn't. Welcome contributions:

## What's welcome

- **Corrections** — typos, broken links, unclear steps.
- **Case studies** — a champion cascade, a board briefing, or a metric that changed a decision. Open an issue first.
- **Metric proposals** — a leading indicator that genuinely predicted adoption, or a vanity metric worth warning against.
- **Translations** — faithful, credited.

## What isn't

- Anything that replaces the accountability question ("where did the saved time go?") with pure activity metrics.
- Vendor or affiliate placements.

## Standards

Plain, specific, UK English. No hype, no "transformation" theatre. Open an issue before large changes; PRs reviewed as promptly as a one-person team reasonably can.
