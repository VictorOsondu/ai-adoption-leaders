# Contributing

This playbook gets better when leaders who've actually run an adoption programme share what worked and what didn't. Welcome contributions:

## What's welcome

- **Corrections** — typos, broken links, unclear steps.
- **Source updates** — changed AI Act, governance, standard, privacy, sector, insurer, or vendor guidance.
- **Case studies** — a champion cascade, a board briefing, or a metric that changed a decision. Open an issue first.
- **Metric proposals** — a leading indicator that genuinely predicted adoption, or a vanity metric worth warning against.
- **Translations** — faithful, credited.

## What isn't

- Anything that replaces the accountability question ("where did the saved time go?") with pure activity metrics.
- Vendor or affiliate placements.
- Board-confidential, client, customer, employee, HR, payroll, financial, legal, strategy, M&A, incident, security, confidential, regulated, proprietary, credential, or personal information in public issues or PRs.

## Standards

Plain, specific, UK English. No hype, no "transformation" theatre. Use public or synthetic examples unless the case is already public. Claims about governance, risk, compliance, tool behaviour, or board reporting should use primary or high-authority sources where possible. Open an issue before large changes; PRs reviewed as promptly as a one-person team reasonably can.
